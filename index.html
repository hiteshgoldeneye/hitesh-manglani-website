About my work

I started in 2008 as a consultant at Capgemini, writing C# and ASP.NET applications, and moved to Dynamics AX Retail at Microsoft in 2011. Since then I have been a technical consultant, product manager and architect on retail projects at Columbus, NEC, KrisShop, IBM, Illumina and Accenture.

These days I build Azure integrations that sync product and price catalogues into ERP. When I solve something that took too long to find, I write it up on the blog so the next person doesn't have to start from scratch.
